Solar System

About the solar system
Solar System definition :
The Solar System is the collection of the Sun and all the objects that orbit around it because of the Sun's gravity. It formed about 4.6 billion years ago from a large cloud of gas and dust.

The Sun

The Sun is a star, not a planet. It contains almost all of the Solar System's mass and provides the energy that makes life on Earth possible. The Sun produces energy through nuclear fusion, mainly converting hydrogen into helium.
