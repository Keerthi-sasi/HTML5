🌌 SOLAR SYSTEM

About the Solar System

The Solar System is made up of the Sun, eight planets, dwarf planets, moons, asteroids, comets and other space objects. The eight planets in order from the Sun are Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune.

The Sun is at the center of the Solar System and provides light and heat to the planets. Each planet follows its own path, called an orbit, around the Sun.

☀️ The Sun

The Sun is the star at the center of our Solar System. It is a huge ball of extremely hot gases and provides light and heat to all the planets.

The Sun is much larger than Earth and contains most of the mass in the Solar System. Its energy makes life possible on Earth and influences the climate and weather of our planet.

🌙 The Moon

The Moon is Earth's natural satellite. It is the closest major celestial object to Earth and takes about one month to orbit our planet.

The Moon has a rocky surface covered with craters, mountains and valleys. Its gravity causes most of the ocean tides on Earth.

1. Mercury

Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun and the smallest planet in our Solar System. It has a rocky surface covered with many craters.

Mercury has a very thin atmosphere and experiences large changes in temperature between day and night. It takes about 88 Earth days to complete one orbit around the Sun.

2. Venus

Venus is the second planet from the Sun. It is similar in size and structure to Earth and has a thick atmosphere.

Venus has a rocky surface covered with mountains and volcanoes. Its thick atmosphere traps heat, making it the hottest planet in the Solar System.

3. Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and our home planet. It has liquid water on its surface and an atmosphere that supports life.

Earth has one natural satellite called the Moon. About 71 percent of Earth's surface is covered by water.

4. Mars

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun. It is known as the Red Planet because of its reddish appearance.

Mars has mountains, valleys, volcanoes and polar ice caps. It has two small moons called Phobos and Deimos.

5. Jupiter

Jupiter is the fifth planet from the Sun and the largest planet in our Solar System. It is a gas giant made mostly of hydrogen and helium.

Jupiter has colorful cloud bands and a large storm called the Great Red Spot. It has many moons and a faint ring system.

6. Saturn

Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun. It is a gas giant famous for its large and beautiful ring system.

Saturn's rings are made mostly of pieces of ice and rock. The planet has many moons and is less dense than Earth.

7. Uranus

Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun. It is an ice giant with a blue-green appearance caused by methane in its atmosphere.

Uranus has a faint ring system and many moons. It rotates at a large tilt, making it appear to rotate on its side.

8. Neptune

Neptune is the eighth and most distant planet from the Sun. It is a cold, blue ice giant with a thick atmosphere.

Neptune has extremely strong winds and a faint ring system. It has several moons, including its largest moon, Triton.

🌍 Planets in Order

Mercury → Venus → Earth → Mars → Jupiter → Saturn → Uranus → Neptune
